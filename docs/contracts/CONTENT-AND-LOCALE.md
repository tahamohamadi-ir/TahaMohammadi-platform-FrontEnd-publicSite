# Content and Locale Contract

Supported locales are Persian (`fa`, RTL) and English (`en`, LTR). Locale is explicit in public routes after the language gateway. A missing translation is a content state, not permission to machine-translate or copy the other locale without an approved fallback.

Names, affiliations, contact details, CV files, biographies, claims, dates, project records, and external identifiers come from approved source content or the backend. Components may format data but may not alter its meaning.
